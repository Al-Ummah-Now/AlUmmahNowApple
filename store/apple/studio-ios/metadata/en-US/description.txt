Al Ummah Now Studio is for creators on Al Ummah Now. Upload videos to your channel and manage them from your iPhone or iPad.

• Pick videos from Photos or Files
• Upload several videos at once, with a title pattern and the same description, category, language, visibility, tags and playlists for all of them
• Schedule videos to publish later; they stay private until their time
• Uploads continue when you leave the app and resume where they stopped
• Edit, delete and restore your videos from Content
• See your channel's numbers in Analytics
• Switch between your accounts and the channels you help run
• Arrange, split and export clips in the editor

Uploading is by invitation: you need an Al Ummah Now account with creator access.
